24/7 Medical & Oxygen Posts

  • SNM District Hospital, Leh: Primary altitude trauma centre & hyperbaric chamber. Tel: +91-1982-252014
  • Diskit Sub-District Hospital (Nubra): 24/7 emergency oxygen & ambulance base. Tel: +91-1982-220022
  • Tangtse Emergency Clinic: Located before Pangong Lake for SpO2 stabilization.
  • BRO Highway Rescue & HIMANK: Emergency road clearance. Tel: 1077
📄

Inner Line Permit (ILP) Checkpoints

  • Carry 4 Physical Hardcopies: Required at South Pullu, North Pullu, Khardung La, and Tsaga La check-posts.
  • Designated ILP Circuits: Nubra Valley, Pangong Tso, Tso Moriri, Turtuk, Tyakshi, and Hanle.
  • Valid Photo ID: Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID required alongside digital LAHDC receipt.
🏔️

AMS Emergency Action Protocol

  • Golden Rule: Never ascend with symptoms of Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS).
  • Symptoms: Throbbing headache, nausea, extreme fatigue, shortness of breath at rest.
  • Action: Descend immediately to Leh (11,500 ft) or nearest low camp. Administer supplemental oxygen.
  • Hydration: 4–5 liters daily with oral rehydration salts (ORS).
🌿

Himalayan Leave-No-Trace Code

  • No Single-Use Bottles: Refill filtered boiled water at Dzomsa eco-stations in Leh.
  • Glacier Streams: No soap or detergents within 100m of alpine streams or lakes.
  • Monastic Respect: Remove shoes, walk clockwise around stupas, and ask prior photo consent.
